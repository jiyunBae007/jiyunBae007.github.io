About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Graduate School of Artificial Intelligence at POSTECH, advised by Jaeho Lee in the Efficient Learning Lab (EffL). My research focuses on the robustness and trustworthiness of vision-language models, especially reasoning VLMs. I am interested in understanding when and why these models fail to perceive the visual world as faithfully as they reason about it, and in building AI systems that do.

Before joining POSTECH, I received my B.S. in Information Security from Seoul Women's University, and worked with EffL as an undergraduate research fellow.
